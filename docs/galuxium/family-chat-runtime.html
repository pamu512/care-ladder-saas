Nobody opens a console when Mom might need help. They open WhatsApp.

Care Ladder for families runs where the family already is: a bot thread per household. It informs with a countdown, takes the acknowledgment in chat (button tap or a one-word reply), sends updates while someone is on it, and only climbs the ladder (you, then Sarah, then a call) when the chat goes quiet. The web console becomes the place you set up once (pairing, contact ladder, cadence) and visit to review.

Step 2 · 5:00Family channel informedWhatsApp/Telegram card with blurred frame, countdown and three buttons. This is the "inform and update" beat.
Step 3 · +3:00Countdown pressure in chat"No acknowledgment in 3:00: calling you in 2:00 unless someone taps." Same thread, escalating tone.
Step 4 · 5:00Voice call to youActual phone call with a spoken summary. If unanswered, next contact after 2:00.

Platform honesty (affects implementation order): Telegram bots can send free-form messages and inline buttons any time: the fastest full experience to ship. WhatsApp requires an approved UTILITY template for business-initiated alerts inside the 24h customer-service window; until approval lands, WhatsApp alerts fall back to a templated text + tappable ack link (the existing signed-token page), and buttons arrive once the template is live. Both channels already have adapters in the codebase (channels/router.py: TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN + TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID, WHATSAPP_TOKEN + WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID). what's new is the conversation state machine: page → countdown → ack → update → close, with every hop recorded in the incident audit trail.
Care Ladder is a wellness companion, not a medical device · never calls emergency services on its own · frames blurred before persisting · demo data throughout.

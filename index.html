The person answers first. Everyone else is plan B.

Before any alert leaves the building, Care Ladder asks the person directly - through a speaker check-in. The response decides what happens next, and the response itself is the product's core signal.

Built to be defensible, not just impressive

Every claim the product makes is one a care operator can stand behind.

Privacy before persistence

Frames are blurred or silhouetted before anything is stored. Person tracking uses shape geometry only - never identity. Clips state their retention window.

Every hop on the record

Cue, check-in, page, acknowledgment, call, close - each step is timestamped in a per-incident audit trail, exportable for review and shift handover.

Fail-closed by design

Emergency numbers are never dialed automatically; the emergency rung ships disabled and stays behind a human gate. Unsigned billing webhooks are rejected outright.

Questions people actually ask

Does it call emergency services by itself?

No. The emergency rung is fail-closed: it ships disabled and never dials on its own. A human always decides. In every demo the numbers dialed are reserved fictional ones.

Is this a medical device?

Care Ladder is a wellness workflow, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, does not score clinical risk, and never presents itself as one.

What happens if nobody acknowledges?

The ladder climbs on timers: after the acknowledgment window expires, the next contact is paged, then called - each step visible in the console and recorded in the audit trail.

What does the camera actually see?

Cues are behavioral: stillness in a zone, a covered lens, a vertical-to-horizontal transition that resembles a fall. Frames are privacy-transformed before persisting, and tracking never identifies individuals.

Can staff step away without breaking it?

Responders set breaks from the staff console; auto-routing shows their status and countdowns, and the floor lead can pull someone off break as a logged override. (Chat-side break commands are on the roadmap.)

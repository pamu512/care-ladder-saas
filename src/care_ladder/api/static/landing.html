Escalation that waits for a human, then acts

When a check-in goes quiet, Care Ladder pages the floor over the channel your team already lives in and pauses for an acknowledgment. One tap on the ack link - from a phone, no login - records who is responding and stops the climb.

No acknowledgment inside the window and the ladder moves up on its own: supervisor page next, then a phone call. Every hop is in the per-incident audit trail.

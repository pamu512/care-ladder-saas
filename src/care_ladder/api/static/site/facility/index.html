Three tabs, three jobs

The console is deliberately quiet: triage what needs a human, track what humans are handling, audit what humans did.

Alert center

Triage + override

Priority queue with aging and SLA countdowns. Assign to available staff (breaks respected), reprioritize, de-escalate, suppress - every override logged with the lead's name.

Cases

Handled in chat, tracked here

Acknowledged cases get owners and handling clocks; over-target cases get SLA nudges; closures require submitted documentation.

Audit

The review surface

Median time-to-ack, handling times, outcome mix - and per-incident drill-downs: full ladder, reply transcript, documentation, overrides, clips. CSV export for compliance.
